03 — Yards
Three Yards · One Group

Three yards on the Great Lakes.

Three integrated facilities on the Great Lakes, each with its own specialty. Together they form the largest shipbuilding capacity in the inland United States.

Marinette, WI

Fincantieri Marinette Marine

Surface combatants and littoral platforms for the US Navy, including the Constellation-class frigate and the Freedom-variant Littoral Combat Ship.

Established 1942
Specialty Naval combatants
Sturgeon Bay, WI

Fincantieri Bay Shipbuilding

Largest commercial shipyard on the Great Lakes. ATBs, self-unloading bulkers, LNG bunker barges, and full-service drydock for vessels up to 1,158 ft.

Established 1918
Specialty Commercial & repair
Green Bay, WI

Fincantieri ACE Marine

Aluminum specialty vessels for law enforcement, military, and commercial customers. Patrol boats, fast response craft, and custom small craft.

Established 1981
Specialty Aluminum specialty craft
