The parent equity is real. The entity unification isn't.

Fincantieri Marine Group is the American operating arm of one of the world's largest shipbuilders. Parent group: 228 years at sea, 1959 reincorporation, 18 yards on four continents, €9.2B in 2025 revenue, €63B order backlog, roughly 24,000 employees. American programs include the four new Medium Landing Ships awarded to Marinette in February 2026, with $800M in congressional support and a program ceiling of up to 35 vessels.

None of that surfaces in Google's structured data layer the way it does for HII, General Dynamics, or Austal USA. Organization schema is not cross-linked to the parent Fincantieri S.p.A. entity. sameAs unification across Wikipedia, Wikidata, the parent IR site, and the three yard subdomains is incomplete. The three yards do not have per-Manufacturer schema. The four active programs do not have program-page schema. AI engines that decide who to shortlist for "American defense shipbuilders" treat the American subsidiary as a separate, smaller entity than the search graph allows.
