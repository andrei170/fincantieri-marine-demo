SEO Buildout · Fincantieri Marine Group 02 — Executive Summary
02 · Executive Summary

A measured search expansion for the commercial side.

Defense contracts move through RFPs. Commercial vessel buyers, by contrast, search. This proposal is built for the second.

Fincantieri Marine Group sits inside a Fincantieri S.p.A. production network with 7,000+ ships across 228 years of yard history, €9.2 billion in 2025 group revenue, and a 97-vessel order book through 2037. The brand authority is overwhelming. The website does not reflect it.

A live audit of fincantierimarinegroup.com surfaces a WordPress + Oxygen build with 2019 to 2021 image quality, a 50+ item mega-menu masking the vessel taxonomy, and a generic contact CTA. For a defense-grade subsidiary, the digital surface lags the brand by roughly half a decade.

The 90-day proposal outlined in this document does not target the Navy or Coast Guard procurement track. It targets the commercial vessel inquiry pipeline: ATB operators searching for shipyards, midstream operators sourcing LNG bunker barge capacity, Great Lakes commercial buyers researching self-unloading bulkers, and the long tail of capability research that precedes a multi-million-dollar conversation.

In one paragraph

Audit and resurface the existing site. Rebuild capability pages as cornerstone content per vessel class. Architect a Wisconsin maritime authority cluster around the three yards. Capture commercial keyword inventory FMG already ranks for, and add the long-tail terms FMG should rank for. Measure inquiry pipeline, not vanity traffic. Defense contracts continue moving through their own channel. This work runs alongside, not against.

Document Scope The proposal that follows is technical and conservative. Search benchmarks are referenced in ranges, not promises. Defense-vertical SEO has limited public benchmarking; commercial maritime is better-documented and forms the basis of this engagement's projection envelope.

SEO Buildout · Fincantieri Marine Group 03 — Current Site Findings
03 · Current Site Findings

Five gaps between the brand and the digital surface.

Audit pass on fincantierimarinegroup.com, May 2026.

Finding 01 · Photography Vessel imagery uploaded 2019 to 2021. Inconsistent crop ratios, weak compression, multiple H2-on-image template artifacts. For a builder of Constellation-class frigates and Polar Security Cutters, the photography fails the brand on first frame. 3 / 10
Finding 02 · Architecture Mega-menu hides 50+ vessel-taxonomy items behind nested dropdowns. No scannable vessel grid on the homepage. Markets surface, but vessel classes beneath them are buried two clicks deep. Bad for users, worse for crawlers. 4 / 10
Finding 03 · Capability Pages Vessel pages exist but are under-built as cornerstone content. Spec sheets are thin. Case-study coverage is light. Each vessel class is a potential top-of-funnel keyword target left thin. 5 / 10
Finding 04 · CTAs Single generic Contact CTA across the site. No per-vessel "Request capabilities document," no "Schedule a yard tour," no segmented intake by market or vessel class. Inquiries flow into one inbox without context. 4 / 10
Finding 05 · Mobile Oxygen Builder defaults render passably, but the mega-menu collapses awkwardly on phones and the small vessel photos look weaker at narrow widths. Mobile-first indexing is the default; the site is desktop-first by design. 5 / 10

Three of these gaps are SEO-critical: the architecture, the capability pages, and the mobile rendering. Two are brand-critical: the photography and the CTA flow. The 90-day buildout addresses all five.

The strongest social-proof asset Fincantieri owns is the parent group. The current site does not lead with it. Visitors land on a generic vessel grid before they learn the company is part of a 228-year, 18-yard, 7,000-ship operation.

A single editorial above-the-fold change, parent-group stat band leading the homepage, lifts perceived authority before any keyword work begins. A reference build of this change is available at andrei170.github.io/fincantieri-marine-demo.

SEO Buildout · Fincantieri Marine Group 04 — Three Buildout Vectors
04 · Three Buildout Vectors

Three concentrated vectors, not a hundred small fixes.

Each vector targets a specific buyer segment FMG already serves. None of them touches the federal RFP track.

Vector01

Commercial Vessel Keyword Expansion

Long-tail commercial buyer terms with high deal-value-per-search. Low competition, low monthly volume, and four-figure to six-figure deal sizes per converted inquiry. The pipeline math works at single-digit monthly searches.

Targets: LNG bunker barge builder · articulated tug barge construction · Great Lakes shipyard · self-unloading bulker shipyard · platform support vessel builder USA · custom commercial vessel design · Jones Act shipbuilder · ship repair Wisconsin · commercial drydock Great Lakes

Vector02

Capability-Page Architecture

Each vessel class becomes a cornerstone page, structured as a top-of-funnel resource. Specs, hull form, propulsion, classification society approvals, sample build references, and a vessel-specific CTA. Pages link laterally to related classes and vertically to the parent yard.

Pages built: Constellation-Class Frigate · Polar Security Cutter · LNG Bunker Barge · Articulated Tug Barge · Platform Support Vessel · Wind Farm Service Vessel · Self-Unloading Bulker · Cutter-Suction Dredge · Passenger Ferry · Repair & Refit · Custom Build · Capabilities Index

Vector03

Wisconsin Maritime Authority Cluster

Three yards, three localised authority hubs. Marinette Marine for naval combatants, Bay Shipbuilding for Great Lakes commercial, ACE Marine for aluminum specialty craft. Each yard hub anchors a content cluster of vessel pages, capability case studies, and regional maritime authority signals (trade press citations, classification society pages, federal contractor directories).

Hubs: Marinette, WI · Sturgeon Bay, WI · Green Bay, WI · plus a unified "Three Yards / One Group" architectural spine that re-uses the parent-group social-proof band on every yard hub.

SEO Buildout · Fincantieri Marine Group 05 — 90-Day Plan & Projection
05 · 90-Day Plan

A phased 90-day plan with visible milestones.

Phase 01Days 1–30

Audit, Foundation, & Quick Wins

Full technical audit. Core Web Vitals pass. Schema markup. Image compression and alt-text rebuild. Sitemap, robots, indexability sweep. Editorial homepage rebuild leading with the parent-group stat band (reference build already available).

  • Technical audit + crawl report (Screaming Frog + manual)
  • Image weight reduction and lazy-load pass
  • Schema rollout: Organization, Product (vessel class), LocalBusiness (per yard)
  • Homepage parent-group lead-in shipped to staging
Phase 02Days 31–60

Capability Architecture & Cornerstone Content

Twelve vessel-class cornerstone pages built and cross-linked. Capability index page architected. Each yard page rebuilt as a localised authority hub. Internal-link graph rewired around vessel-class cornerstones.

  • 12 cornerstone vessel pages, ~1,800–2,400 words each
  • Three yard authority hubs (Marinette, Sturgeon Bay, Green Bay)
  • Per-vessel CTA templates (capabilities document, yard tour, custom RFI)
  • Internal-link audit and rewire
Phase 03Days 61–90

Authority Signals, Tracking, & Reporting

Trade press outreach for citations. Classification society and Jones Act directory listings. Federal contractor directory hygiene. GA4 + Search Console wired to inquiry-attribution dashboard. Monthly performance reporting cadence locked.

  • 10–15 trade press / industry directory citation acquisitions
  • Inquiry attribution (vessel class, yard, source) live in dashboard
  • Position-tracking baseline locked across the keyword inventory
  • Month-3 report and quarter-2 roadmap
